# Use RapidPlan Online on a mobile phone

You can use RapidPlan Online and RapidPath Online on a mobile phone. The touch controls let you navigate and edit a plan without a mouse.

![RapidPlan Online displayed on a mobile phone](./use-rapidplan-online-on-mobile.png)

For available gestures, see [Use mobile devices](../using-mobile-devices/use-mobile-devices).
